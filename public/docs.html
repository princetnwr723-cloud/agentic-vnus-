What is Agentic Vnus?

Agentic Vnus is a self-hosted AI agent that runs entirely on your PC. It connects your browser dashboard to an always-available AI that controls your machine — files, browser, terminal, GitHub, and more.

Who is it for? Developers, founders, and power users who want a personal AI that knows their work, executes on their machine, and gets smarter every day — without sending data to any cloud.

How It Works

Commands from your browser reach the agent via Firebase Realtime Database WebSocket — instant delivery, zero polling. Local AI processes the command, plans actions, executes them on your PC.

→

→

→

Zero polling

Unlike Firestore polling (3 second delay), RTDB WebSocket delivers commands in under 100ms. 99% less API calls.

Installation

Installation Warnings

Agentic Vnus is not yet code-signed by Microsoft or Apple. Your OS will show a security warning during installation. This is completely normal — the app is safe.

Windows — "Don't Run" Warning

Windows SmartScreen

"Windows protected your PC" appears because the app is not yet Microsoft-certified. Click More info → Run anyway.

  • 1
    Download the .exe
    If browser blocks download, click the arrow → Keep anyway
  • 2
    Click "More info"
    On the blue SmartScreen warning, click More info at the bottom
  • 3
    Click "Run anyway"
    Installation begins. Click Yes on the UAC prompt that follows.

Mac — "Cannot be Opened" Warning

macOS Gatekeeper

"Agentic Vnus cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer." App is safe — not yet Apple-notarized.

  • 1
    Download the correct DMG
    Intel Mac: AgenticVnus-1.0.0-x64.dmg · Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4): AgenticVnus-1.0.0-arm64.dmg
  • 2
    Right-click → Open
    Do NOT double-click. Right-click the app in Applications → Open
  • 3
    Click "Open" in popup
    Only needed first time. Opens normally every time after that.
  • 4
    If no Open button
    System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → Open Anyway

Terminal alternative: sudo xattr -rd com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Agentic Vnus.app"

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the agent work without internet?

Yes — after first setup, everything runs offline. Only network activity is the Realtime Database WebSocket to receive commands from your dashboard. AI processing is entirely local.

Where does my data go?

Commands temporarily pass through Realtime Database to bridge browser and PC. AI processing happens entirely on your machine. Memory, skills, session history, tokens — stored locally, never transmitted anywhere.

What if model download fails?

Agent shows specific error and Retry button. Your plan stays active. Check internet connection and available disk space — you need at least 2 GB more than the model size.

Can I use the agent on multiple PCs?

Yes — each PC gets its own connection code. Add as many workspaces as your plan allows. Each workspace runs independently with its own model and memory.

Does the scheduler keep running when browser is closed?

Yes — scheduler runs inside the Electron agent in your system tray. As long as the tray icon is visible, all scheduled tasks execute on time regardless of browser state.

What is Business DNA and how long does setup take?

Business DNA is a one-time 20-question setup (about 5 minutes) where you introduce your business. After that, your agent remembers everything forever and acts as a dedicated AI executive in your chosen role.

Can I reset Business DNA?

Yes — type "update my business DNA" or go to the Business DNA section in your workspace dashboard and click "Update Business DNA".

Technical Reference

Architecture

brain.js — Intelligence Layer

Builds system prompt combining persistent memory, skills, business DNA context, and task-specific Chain-of-Thought reasoning. Detects task type (coding / browser / files / reasoning) and routes to appropriate model. Responses parsed as structured JSON action sequences. Invalid output retries 3 times.

businessDNA.js — Business Context

Handles 20-question setup flow. Stores DNA at ~/.vnus-agent/business-dna.json. Injects business context into every AI prompt. Powers role-based agent modes. Source for all proactive features.

multiAgent.js — Team System

Boss Agent detects complex tasks and selects team template. 8 specialized agents with distinct system prompts. Sequential execution with context passing between agents. Results compiled by Editor Agent.

proactiveAgent.js — Proactive Intelligence

Generates morning briefings, opportunity scans, and weekly reports using business DNA context. All results synced to RTDB for dashboard display.

scheduler.js — Cron Engine

node-cron engine for 6 trigger types. All operations received via RTDB WebSocket instantly from website. History logged to ~/.vnus-agent/schedule-history.json. Retry with exponential backoff.

listener.js — Command Delivery

Firebase Realtime Database SSE WebSocket — zero polling. Commands arrive instantly. Rate limited 20/min per user. Ownership verified before execution.
